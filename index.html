Hoop & Disc

Catch with the hoop. Never with the disc.

Hoop & Disc is a one-finger arcade game. Drag the disc and the hoop chases it. Sweep up targets with the hoop, but never let a target touch the disc. Power-ups keep changing the rules, so stay sharp.

Simple to play, hard to master

One finger, two pieces

Drag the disc. The hoop follows wherever it goes.

Catch, don't touch

Catch targets with the hoop. One touch on the disc and it's over.

7 rule-changing power-ups

Shields, triple hoops, an invisible hoop, a runaway disc and more.

Kamikaze targets

Red targets dive straight at your disc. Dodge them or catch them.

6 themes

Purple, Ocean, Neon, Ice, Sunset and Classic. Pick your look.

No pop-up ads

Ads only when you choose: for an extra life or to unlock a theme.
