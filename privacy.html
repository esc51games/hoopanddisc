Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Hoop & Disc ("the game", "we") uses, why, and what choices you have. In short: the game itself does not collect or send any personal information. The only third party is Google AdMob, which shows the optional ads.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few things on your own device so it can remember them between sessions:

  • your scores,
  • the visual theme you selected,
  • the time a theme you unlocked with an ad will lock again.

This information never leaves your device through the game. We do not run any servers and do not receive it. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos or any account.

If your device's own backup feature is turned on (for example Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad), the operating system may include this game data in your backup. That backup is handled by your device's provider, not by us.

Advertising

The game has no pop-up ads. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one, for example to get an extra life or to unlock a theme. These ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking, see below),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with the ads.

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google's own policies. We do not receive or store it. You can read more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices

  • Watching ads is always optional. You can play the whole game without watching any ad.
  • If you are in a region where consent is required by law (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later in the game.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for your permission before Google can use your device's advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to you. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device; on iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • You can delete all game data stored on your device at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
